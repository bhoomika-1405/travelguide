💌 Behind The Paper

About TravelGuide 🎀

The story of a cozy, hand-drawn travel notebook for modern explorers.

Our Little Philosophy

Travel is meant to feel like a cherished journal, not a boardroom meeting.

We built TravelGuide for dreamers who prefer pastel stickers, washi tape corners, and relaxed café afternoon breaks over rushed 20-stop tourist marathons.

Whether you are exploring quiet alleyways in Kyoto, watching pastel sunsets in Santorini, or sipping espresso beside the Seine, your itinerary should be joyful to look at and effortless to plan.
